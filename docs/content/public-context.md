## E15 / Form 15 (Sudan, Ministry of Finance)

- Form 15 (أورنيك 15) was the paper receipt used for all payments to government, managed by the Federal Ministry of Finance. — SUST repository thesis (2006): https://repository.sustech.edu/handle/123456789/7359
- Paper Form-15 transactions were declared invalid from end of June 2015, with electronic collection rolling out by state (e.g. North Darfur; Blue Nile from 2 July 2015). — Dabanga: https://dabangasudan.org/en?p=182643 · Radio Tamazuj: https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/blue-nile-introduces-new-electronic-form-for-revenue-collection
- A 2015–2020 study found electronic collection increased internal control and reduced collection costs, alongside technical and human implementation problems. — Al-Neelain repository: https://repository.neelain.edu.sd/items/b9dd8f20-3c5f-4dc2-98c1-65d6431c454b
- Scale claims (6,000+ offices, 51,000+ users, 21,000+ services) appear only on a third party's LinkedIn profile — **do not use**.

## Tanfeeth (Saudi Central Bank)

- SAMA launched the "Tanfeeth" program on 14 February 2019 to link government systems directly with banks; the Ministry of Justice's execution system was the first connection. — SAMA: https://sama.gov.sa/en-US/News/Pages/news14022019.aspx
- Later connections include GOSI and the Saudi Business Center (2022) and the Board of Grievances (2023). — SAMA: https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-767.aspx

## sarie / IPS (Saudi Payments)

- Saudi Payments launched the instant payment system "sarie" in April 2021 with IBM and Mastercard, available to customers of all Saudi banks. — IBM newsroom: https://newsroom.ibm.com/2021-04-21-Saudi-Payments-Launches-Instant-Payments-System-sarie-in-Cooperation-with-IBM-and-Mastercard
- May 2023: 35.13 million instant transactions in the month (SAMA bulletin via Arab News). — https://www.arabnews.pk/business/saudi-banking-platform-sarie-sees-29-jump-in-customer-payment-to-286bn-in-may-2331311
- Financial Sector Development Program target: 70% non-cash transactions by 2030. — Intelligent CIO: https://www.intelligentcio.com/me/2021/04/22/saudi-payments-unveils-instant-payments-system-sarie/
